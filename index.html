SOP excerpt: supplier invoice check

Sample
  1. Open the invoice and the job's approved material list side by side.
  2. Match each line by item, size and quantity.
  3. Compare unit price to the quoted price; note any difference over 2%.
  4. Log mismatches in the job notes with the invoice number.
  5. Email the supplier for a corrected invoice; flag the owner only if the job cost changes.
  6. Enter the approved costs in the job budget and attach the invoice.

Written so a new VA can follow it without asking.
